# Manychat comment -> DM workflow

## Prerequisites
Instagram professional account (Creator or Business) and connected Manychat account. In Manychat: Automation -> New -> Post/Reel comments -> Specific Reel (preferred) -> include keyword DIRECTOR -> opening private reply. Check documented Meta connection permissions. Avoid generic automatic replies to every comment.

## Public reply variations (optional, rotate responsibly)
1. "Sent the starter workflow to your DMs—hope it helps with your next edit."
2. "Just shared the director breakdown. The setup steps are included."
3. "Check your DMs for the free starter kit and scene example."

## Opening DM — message (one block)
"Here’s the free Video Director Starter you asked for. Want the editable workflow + example scene?"
Quick reply: "Send my starter".

**On opt-in click** (within platform allowed messaging window):
"Here you go: [REAL_FREEBIE_URL]. It includes the original starter skill, one working Remotion scene, storyboard, SFX cues and setup notes. Which part would you like to learn first: story, motion or sound?"

**Only after a meaningful user reply** (optional, contextual):
Offer a short help answer first. Then, if relevant: "I'm also building a deeper paid studio toolkit with more complete recipes and QA checks; want the details when it's ready?" Do not forcibly upsell or assume consent to broadcast.

## Critical platform details
Manychat says an initial private reply does NOT itself opt the commenter into general conversations or open a 24-hour messaging window. A quick reply/button interaction can initiate that. External-website button alone does not count as opting into conversation. Also the comment trigger normally fires only once per user per Reel. Check latest Manychat documentation before launch.

# Brand bible — working direction

## Promise
"Cinema, engineered." The audience gets original cinematic proof and an executable creator workflow. The brand earns authority through reproducibility, not hidden tricks.

## Audience
Primary: freelance editors, motion designers, creative agencies and creators already curious about Claude Code. Secondary: SaaS founders and marketers who require repeatable high-end video production.

## Art system
- Canvas: 9:16, normally 1080×1920; 30 fps, H.264 MP4 + AAC.
- Visual identity: cinematic editorial minimalism, black/charcoal environment, warm off-white text and a single vivid accent (tentative high-saturation chartreuse or electric coral). Restrained premium materials, real light, depth and grain where justified.
- Type: one well-licensed display grotesk and one functional sans/mono; don't ship unlicensed commercial fonts.
- Motion: designed from physical causes. Speed variation / held stillness / motivated match action. A movement should create meaning, not fill time.
- Recurring visual motif: a line/film-strip/selection box that becomes a frame, then a shot, then a rendered artifact. Flexes without repetitive template feel.
- Sound logo: quiet tactile click -> subtle low-end pulse -> resolving air. Distinct but not on every edit.
- SFX materials: mechanical snaps for interfaces, tactile texture for materials, spatial ambience for environments, silence for reveals.
- Reels should stand without audio for the main idea, but improve with sound.

## Copy system
- Speak like a confident director, not a viral hack seller.
- Never open with "Did you know" or "This will blow your mind".
- Demonstrate work before explaining it.
- CTA appears late and promises the specific free item. Use simple keywords (DIRECTOR, SOUND, CUT).
- Avoid claims such as "100% made with Claude" if any footage, 3D, sound or manual editing was contributed elsewhere.

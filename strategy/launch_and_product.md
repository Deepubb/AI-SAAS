# Instagram-first studio launch: 30 days

## Goal
Validate whether film-quality Claude Code workflows attract the intended audience and whether those viewers actively request and use a free starter kit. Initial success is not vanity follower count; it is credible proof, downloads and hands-on workflow usage.

## Content cadence
Publish 3 high-quality Reels per week to start (12 per 4 weeks) instead of 7 generic Reels. Reuse finished animation components, but original stories and edits. Increase frequency only after process quality is stable. Maintain 1–2 casual behind-the-scenes Stories on non-post days if capacity allows.

## Four pillars and relative mix
- 40% PROOF — finished extraordinary edit followed by brief workflow reveal.
- 25% BREAKDOWN — exactly one editorial choice explained visually.
- 20% EXPERIMENT — same asset or scene transformed 2–3 ways.
- 15% TRUST — real mistakes, fixes, process, free starter evidence and product update.

## First 12 Reel ideas
1. The Director Is a File — comment DIRECTOR (lead magnet).
2. Same footage, two sound mixes — comment SOUND (SFX starter cues).
3. The match cut you didn't see — comment CUT (cut recipe).
4. One shape transforms three scenes — comment MATCH (recipe + editable example).
5. 4 frames that killed the punchline — comment TIMING (timing breakdown).
6. From storyboard to finished film — comment BOARD (storyboard).
7. Why this slow camera push feels expensive — comment MOTION (easing recipe).
8. One type treatment, three emotions — comment TYPE (type system).
9. The invisible cut — comment CUT (variant with distinct demo).
10. My Claude render failed 6 tests — comment QA (actual defect list + checks).
11. Build a complete 15-second product reveal — comment REVEAL (free partial recipe).
12. Every layer of the final studio system — comment STUDIO (waitlist or real paid details only when ready).

## Metrics (track weekly)
For content: 3s retention where available, average watch time, retention curve, completes/replays where supported, shares per reach, saves per reach, follows per unique viewer. For funnel: keyword comments, successful DM deliveries, opt-ins, link clicks, starter downloads, asset usage, email signups if explicitly obtained, sales. Look for viewers who actually make something after the free kit.

Do not equate comment counts with strong reach or demand. A Reel with less reach but many working starter users may signal a better product fit.

## Freebie and product ladder — hypotheses to test
Free: usable Video Director Starter (installable mini-skill, working scene, shot/sound examples, setup walkthrough).
Core paid: $29–$59 one-time **Founder Toolkit**, includes original skill stack integration, 5–10 demonstrable complete recipes, organized source project, sound templates, troubleshooting and licensed asset guide. Prices are test hypotheses, not market-verified.
Premium later: $89–$149 **Studio System**, once there is verified customer demand and more worked examples. Include complete projects and clearly bounded updates. Avoid ongoing subscriptions until regular new releases justify recurrence.

Whop can support digital tools and content delivery. Exact offer/fees should be verified in the seller dashboard before publishing.

## Product ownership
Sell your own authored skill text, project templates, code, asset manifests and tutorials. Give clear license notices for original and third-party materials. Do not simply charge for repackaged EYECANDY content, cloned benchmark animation, Apache/MIT code without required notices, proprietary fonts, or copyrighted footage. Explain any external Claude, Remotion, Blender, SFX or other paid dependencies honestly.

## Pre-launch checklist
1. Verify account name, handle availability, and brand references do not imply Anthropic sponsorship.
2. Establish original repeatable brand art direction and production templates.
3. Produce one genuinely impressive Reel plus its functioning free starter download.
4. Complete a second Reel so the account demonstrates range on launch.
5. Connect Manychat to professional Instagram, test keyword automation and links.
6. Set three pinned posts (proof Reel, process breakdown, actual free starter demo) as posts become available.
7. Track signals after each release, not one-off predictions.
8. Launch paid Whop bundle after users show they can use free kit and request deeper tools; don't market a non-existent toolkit as immediately available.
